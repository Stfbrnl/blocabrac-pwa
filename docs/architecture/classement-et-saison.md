## Cross-user visibility: the `classement_profiles` mirroring pattern

Firestore rules only let a client read their own `users` doc — clients cannot list other clients' results directly. Anywhere the app needs to show one client data derived from another (leaderboard, friends' status), the *source* client mirrors a small summary onto a separate, broadly-readable doc keyed by their own uid (e.g. `classement_profiles/{uid}`), computed and written by themselves on their own activity. Reuse this pattern — do not try to grant broader read rules on `users` or per-user result collections to solve a "show me other users' data" need.

⚠️ **Mirror only what the reader actually consumes, never a whole source field "because it's already there".** `classement_profiles` briefly carried the client's raw `dateOfBirth` (added alongside the field's introduction in `users`, mirrored by habit rather than by need) even though its only reader (`ClientClassement.tsx`) has always just needed a derived FFME age category, never the exact date — an unforced exposure of minors' birth dates to every authenticated account, found and fixed 2026-08-17 (`docs/suivi/SUIVI-date-de-naissance.md`/`docs/handoffs/RETOUR-date-de-naissance-avant-modifs.md`, `docs/handoffs/HANDOFF-date-de-naissance-2026-08-17.md`). Fixed by writing the derived `ffmeCategory` string instead (still computed with `getFfmeCategory(getSeasonAge(...))`, just computed at write time rather than read time) and never writing `dateOfBirth` to this collection again — restricting the read rule was rejected, since the leaderboard's whole reason to exist is reading other clients' profiles. Before adding any new field to a mirrored doc, ask what the reader needs, not what the source doc happens to have.


## `classement_profiles`: incremental counter, never a full-history recompute

`classement_profiles/{uid}`'s score fields (`score`, `bouldersValidated`, `bestColorRank`, `colorCounts`) are maintained **incrementally** by `ClientDaily.tsx` — a validation reads only *its own* prior state (a single `getDoc` on `client_boulder_results/{uid}_{boulderId}`, deterministic ID) and applies a delta inside a `runTransaction`, never a `getDocs` over the client's full history. This replaced an earlier design (V2.26–V2.28) that preloaded the entire success history into memory at mount — correct, but its read cost grew unboundedly with account age, the one metric in the whole app that got worse over time by itself (see `docs/plans/CONCEPTION-selecteur-marge-compteur-incremental.md` §3 for the full reasoning).

- `colorCounts: Partial<Record<Level, number>>` is the **only** source of truth for `bouldersValidated`/`bestColorRank` — both are *derived* from it (`summaryFromColorCounts` in `utils/classementScore.ts`), never incremented independently, so there's only one place drift can originate. `score` deltas are computed by `scoreDeltaForValidation` (old points vs new points for that one boulder) and applied via the transaction's read-then-write, not `increment()` — needed because `bestColorRank` requires reading the current `colorCounts` anyway to recompute after the delta.
- ⚠️ **Re-grading an already-validated boulder retroactively rewrites the score of everyone who validated it** (`docs/plans/PLAN-cloture-ce-soir-et-recotation.md` §2.4), precisely because `client_boulder_results` stores no colour. The stored incremental counter keeps the old value, but the next reconciliation recomputes with the **current** colour and moves everyone at once. A re-grade is therefore a **classement operation, not a form edit**: do it outside a running season (otherwise it also shifts `season.*`), run the reconciliation in simulation first and check the drift covers *exactly* the climbers who validated that boulder and nobody else, then announce it to climbers in the changelog. Watch one specific risk: a climber whose best colour was the old one gains a higher colour, which awards the automatic badge, which since V2.54 drives `users.level` — their level can rise without them climbing anything new, and level drives the weekly missions and the Roulette. Check it before, not after. Done once on 02/10/2026 (n°4 Grande Face, bleu → violet, 7 climbers, +100 points each, no level change because all 7 already had violets).
- The color used for a validation's points is always the boulder's **current** color (`colorById`, from the currently-active `boulders` fetched at mount), never a value frozen at validation time — `client_boulder_results` doesn't store color at all. Recoloring a boulder therefore no longer retroactively updates every past validation's contribution on next page load (the old full-recompute design did this side-effect by accident); only that boulder's *next* validation picks up the new color. This is a deliberate trade-off, not a bug — see the reconciliation script below for the tool to resync if a bulk recolor ever needs to be reflected immediately.
- A deactivated boulder (`is_active: false`) drops out of `ClientDaily.tsx`'s `colorById` (fetched active-only at mount), so a previously-validated-then-deactivated boulder's contribution **freezes** in the stored counter — it is never subtracted. Since V2.56 the reconciliation script agrees: **both `score`/`colorCounts` (all-time) and `season.*` are accumulation counters — a validation of a boulder that has since been rotated out still counts**, at the boulder's last-known colour (`reconcile-classement-profiles.js`'s `loadDailyColorById` has no `is_active` filter). Before V2.56 the monthly reconciliation stripped those points (`--360 points, 1 rouge on a since-deactivated boulder` in the V2.53 log) — a silent decrease nobody could explain, and inconsistent with the stored counter that already froze them. The boulder *doc* is never deleted (rotation is `updateDoc(..., {is_active:false})`), so its colour stays readable.
- **`scripts/reconcile-classement-profiles.js`** is the non-optional safety net for any incremental counter (per this project's own convention, see `cleanup-orphan-boulder-images.js`): recomputes the expected profile from `client_boulder_results` (the real source of truth) for every account in `users` — not every doc in `classement_profiles`, since a legacy account created before this mirroring pattern existed may have **no** profile doc at all (this was true for all 12 real accounts in prod until this script's `--fix` backfilled the one with actual validated results on 2026-08-16 — worth rerunning occasionally, not just after a suspected drift). Dry-run by default, `--fix` to write, `--uid <uid>` to check a single account. **`colorCounts` write removes orphan keys (V2.53)**: corrections go out as `set(update, { merge: true })`, which *merges* maps and never removes a key — so a stale colour key (e.g. a boulder recoloured `"noire"` → `"noir"`, leaving `colorCounts.noire` behind) survived every pass and the profile stayed "en écart" forever. `colorCountsWriteValue(stored, expected)` now adds an explicit `FieldValue.delete()` for every key in stored but not expected, for both `colorCounts` and `season.colorCounts`. Emulator regression test: `frontend/test/reconcile-orphan-key-emulator.mjs`. Runs automatically via `.github/workflows/reconcile-classement-profiles.yml` (monthly cron + `workflow_dispatch`, same pattern as `cleanup-orphan-boulder-images.yml`), journaling every drift found to `cleanup-state/classement-profiles-reconcile-log.json` (committed back by the workflow) whether or not anything gets written. **Drop-guard** (added 2026-08-16 on review feedback): the diff pass always runs *before* any write, and `--fix` aborts with no writes and a non-zero exit (visible workflow failure) if drift affects more than 30% of accounts **and** at least 3 accounts — the original "a drift is corrective by construction" reasoning only holds for a genuine data drift, not for a bug in the reconciliation script's own recompute (or an unreflected barème change), which would otherwise get silently propagated to every profile by the monthly cron. `--force` bypasses the guard explicitly (same escape hatch as `cleanup-orphan-boulder-images.js`); `--uid` (single-account debugging) skips it too, since a lone account has no "proportion" to evaluate. **Journal jamais partagé avec l'émulateur** (retour ClaudeNav 2026-08-19, docs/processus/PROCESSUS-erreurs-avalees.md) : the log path is derived from `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` — when set (any local e2e run that invokes this script, e.g. `e2e-season-classement-flow.mjs`), it writes to `classement-profiles-reconcile-log.emulator.json` instead, gitignored (`cleanup-state/.gitignore`), never the tracked production log. Found the hard way: a local e2e run had already overwritten the real log with emulator uids once, caught only by chance via `git status` before a commit. **Same guard extended to `cleanup-orphan-boulder-images.js`'s `state.json`/`orphan-images-log.json`** (asked for explicitly by ClaudeNav, risk judged *more* severe there since `state.json` directly feeds that script's drop-guard comparison against the previous run — an emulator-polluted reference count could make the guard fire on nothing or, worse, miss a real drop) — verified by hashing `state.json` before/after a real dry-run against the emulator, unchanged. **`compute-classement-saison.js` has no local tracked state/log file at all** (checked, not assumed) — everything it reports goes through `console.log` (captured by the CI run log, never a committed file) and its real writes land in Firestore itself, already correctly scoped to whichever project the Admin SDK is pointed at; nothing to extend this guard to there. None of the three `.github/workflows/*.yml` set `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` (confirmed by grep, not assumed) — in CI all three scripts always write their real production state/log files. ⚠️ **And since 02/10/2026 — *« Une simulation n'écrit rien, nulle part. »*** (a dry run writes nothing, anywhere). The emulator guard above covers "wrong target"; it did not cover "right target, wrong *mode*" — a plain simulation against prod rewrote the tracked production log, destroying the monthly cron's committed run (caught on 02/10 via `git status`, the 01/10 run restored with `git checkout`). The journal is now written only under `--fix`; a simulation prints its detail to the console, which is the correct output for a simulation. Verified empirically after the change: a full dry run against prod leaves `git status cleanup-state/` empty.


## Seasonal classement (`season.*`) and the end-of-season Finale — a second counter, not a replacement

`classement_profiles/{uid}.season.{colorCounts,score}` (V2.4x, see `docs/plans/CONCEPTION-classement-saisonnier.md`) is a **second, independent incremental counter**, added deliberately alongside the all-time one above rather than replacing it — a seasonal bound on the all-time fields was already evaluated and rejected in V2.26 (it would have understated lifetime `score`/`bouldersValidated`, which are meant to track progression forever). The season counter exists for a different purpose: determining who qualifies for a "Finale de l'année" (the small `officiel`/FFME-mode competition, see below), which needs a bounded time window, not a lifetime total.

- **Season window is admin-configurable, never hardcoded** — `app_config/classement_saison` (`debut`/`fin`, ISO dates, plus `cloturee: boolean`), edited via `AdminSeasonConfig.tsx` (`/admin/season-config`). This exists because the app's actual launch date didn't align with the conventional "1er septembre" season start, and rather than hardcode a date and special-case the first season, the window is just data the admin sets once. `ClientDaily.tsx` reads it once at mount (a single `getDoc`, cached in a ref — never per-validation) and applies the *same* delta already computed for the all-time counter (`scoreDeltaForValidation`/`summaryFromColorCounts`, see `utils/classementScore.ts`) to `season.*` as well, but **only if "now" falls within `[debut, fin]`** (`isWithinSeasonWindow`, pure and unit-tested). Outside the window (summer, or no window configured yet), only the all-time counter moves — a grimpeur's personal progression stat never stops accumulating, only the seasonal qualification counter pauses.
- **Calendar and the "saison à venir" phase (V2.71, `docs/handoffs/RETOUR-v2701-v2702.md` §4)**: the user chose seasons running **1 November → 31 May**, with June kept for the Finale. The first season is 2026-11-01 → 2027-05-31, and the user sets it themself. A window saved weeks ahead creates a fourth state beyond absent / open / closed: **configured but not open yet**.
  - `seasonPhase(config, todayISO)` in `utils/classementScore.ts` (pure, tested) returns `'aucune' | 'a_venir' | 'en_cours' | 'terminee'`.
  - `ClientClassement.tsx` reads `app_config/classement_saison` once at mount. In season mode it shows the dates and "le classement général compte dès maintenant" instead of a table of zeros for every phase other than `en_cours`. `AdminSeasonConfig.tsx`'s chip distinguishes "à venir" from "en cours".
- **⚠️ "Enregistrer" vs "Redémarrer la saison"**: they are not two ways to open a season.
  - `recomputeSeasonBaseline` **ignores dates**: it receives none. "Redémarrer" therefore credits every climber's *entire* success history as the starting score. It never gives zero, whatever `debut` is (unit-tested, because ClaudeNav's review assumed the opposite).
  - A fresh season where everyone starts at zero is opened with **"Enregistrer"**. The admin screen warns about this next to the restart button.
  - **A zero-start season still has a baseline of zero (V2.71.2, `docs/handoffs/RETOUR-v271.md` §2).** The reconciliation skips `season.*` whenever `season.baseScore` is *absent* (`hasBase = baseScore !== undefined`, so 0 is a valid base). Without a base, the season classement would run seven months with no safety net. Two paths close this, both through `seasonZeroBasePatch` in `utils/classementScore.ts` (pure, tested):
    - "Enregistrer" (`AdminSeasonConfig.tsx`) first writes `season.baseScore = 0` / `baseColorCounts = {}` on every profile **that has no base yet**, then the window. It never touches an existing base (a restart credit, or a zero already written), so it is idempotent and a mid-season date edit changes nothing.
    - The shared debounced flush (`buildClassementFlushWrites`) adds the same zero base to a profile's **first season delta** when it has none. This covers accounts created after the window was saved.
    - Verified end to end by `e2e-season-classement-flow.mjs`: the save sets the base on a base-less profile and leaves a 300 credit alone; the flush sets it on a new profile; a forged `season.score` drift is caught and fixed by `reconcile --fix --uid`.
- **Off-season (June → October) is a legitimate state, not an error** (V2.71.2, `docs/handoffs/RETOUR-v271.md` §4). `RECONFIGURATION_GRACE_DAYS` went from 7 to **150**: no summer-long daily red, which would teach everyone to ignore reds. The workflow still fails around 29 October if the next window has still not been saved; that is when a miss would really cost validations. The number is tied to the 1 November start, so shorten it if the season ever starts earlier. Auto-chaining the next window at close (ClaudeNav §4.2) was deliberately deferred.
- **`classementOptIn` gates qualification too, not just public visibility**: a grimpeur who opts out of the public classement (`ClientProfile.tsx`) is also excluded from the season's top-10 selection, even at a qualifying score — refusing the public classement is treated as refusing the Finale too (decided explicitly, not a side effect nobody chose). The code still behaves this way (`compute-classement-saison.js`).
- **⚠️ Client-facing Finale texts are withdrawn until the Finale is confirmed** (user decision, 25/09/2026: "il faut d'abord voir sa faisabilité réelle à l'instant T"). V2.71.1 removed or neutralised three texts. The V2.71 changelog also never mentions the Finale.
  - The Finale **machinery** (archive, `cloturee`, roster button, `officiel` mode) is untouched; only the promise to climbers was removed.
  - The day the Finale is confirmed, put these texts back verbatim, invert step 5 of `e2e-season-classement-flow.mjs` (it currently asserts the profile caption is **absent**), and announce it in the changelog:
  1. `ClientClassement.tsx`, season-mode caption, after "…seuls les blocs réussis pour la première fois pendant la saison comptent": « — ce classement détermine les qualifiés pour la Finale de fin de saison. »
  2. `ClientHelp.tsx`, "Classement des grimpeurs" section, as its own item: « Le classement de saison détermine les qualifiés pour la Finale de fin de saison — désactiver votre apparition au classement vous retire aussi de cette qualification. »
  3. `ClientProfile.tsx`, caption under the "Apparaître dans le classement des grimpeurs" switch: « Désactiver ce réglage vous retire aussi de la qualification pour la Finale de fin de saison. »
  - Same pass, **not** Finale-related, so do not revert it: the help line "Au démarrage d'une saison, votre score de saison repart de vos validations des blocs actuellement en place…" described the credit-carrying "Redémarrer". It now says everyone starts from zero, matching the "Enregistrer" season the user chose.
- **Live season standings cost zero extra reads**: `ClientClassement.tsx` already does one unfiltered `getDocs` over `classement_profiles` for the all-time leaderboard; `season.score`/`season.colorCounts` live on the same documents, so a "Classement de saison" toggle is purely a client-side re-sort/re-render of data already in memory, not a second query.
- **End of season**: `scripts/compute-classement-saison.js` runs daily via `.github/workflows/compute-classement-saison.yml --fix` (a daily cron rather than one fixed date, precisely because the window is admin-configurable and unpredictable) but only actually acts once `fin` has passed **and** `cloturee` isn't already `true` — otherwise it's a no-op, checked every run. **Simulation by default, `--fix` to write** (ClaudeNav review, 2026-08-17): this is the most destructive of the project's three maintenance scripts (an irreversible reset), so it follows the same dry-run-unless-told-otherwise convention as `reconcile-classement-profiles.js`/`cleanup-orphan-boulder-images.js` rather than the inverted default it originally shipped with — without `--fix` it prints the computed top 10/10 and how many profiles would be zeroed, writing nothing. When it does act (`--fix`): separates opted-in profiles by `gender`, ranks by `(season.score desc, totalBouldersSeason desc)` — the tie-break the user chose — takes the top 10 of each (all ties at the cutoff included, never an arbitrary cut), writes the result to `classement_saisons/{"YYYY-YYYY"}` (read-only to clients, write-only via the Admin SDK — nothing in `firestore.rules` opens a client write path to it), sets `cloturee: true` + `cloturee_at` (ISO timestamp), and **only then** resets every profile's `season.*` to zero — archive-then-reset, never the reverse, because unlike the all-time counter's reconciliation ("a drift is corrective by construction"), a premature reset here would be real, unrecoverable data loss.
- **`cloturee` is the guard that stops the monthly all-time reconciliation from undoing the season reset**: if `reconcile-classement-profiles.js` recomputed `season.*` from `client_boulder_results` while `cloturee` is still `true` (the admin hasn't reconfigured next season's window yet), it would see the just-reset zeros as "drift" against the *old* `[debut, fin]` still on record and "correct" them back — silently undoing the reset. So the reconciliation script skips `season.*` (and only that) entirely whenever `cloturee` is `true`; reconfiguring the window (which the admin does as one normal save in `AdminSeasonConfig.tsx`, clearing `cloturee_at` too via `deleteField()`) clears the flag and re-enables it. The same script also reconciles `season.*`/`gender` against `client_boulder_results`/`users` under the same dry-run/`--fix`/drift-guard discipline as the all-time fields — a validation counts toward a season based on its `client_boulder_results.createdAt`, which is why that field had to first be made immutable (see below) before this reconciliation could be trusted at all.
- **A forgotten reconfiguration is otherwise silent** (ClaudeNav review, 2026-08-17): the symptom — everyone at zero in the "Classement de saison" tab — is indistinguishable from a normal season start, and by the time anyone notices, the missed validations can't be recovered (reconciliation only ever recomputes against the *current* window). `AdminSeasonConfig.tsx` shows a warning banner whenever `cloturee` is `true`. That alone isn't enough — nobody reads the logs of a cron that stays green — so `compute-classement-saison.js` also fails the workflow loudly (non-zero exit) once `cloturee_at` is more than `RECONFIGURATION_GRACE_DAYS` days old, rather than silently no-op-ing forever. That was 7 days until V2.71.2, now 150 (see the off-season bullet above).
- **`client_boulder_results.createdAt` immutability was a prerequisite, not a side project**: before this chantier, `createdAt` was silently reset to "now" on every edit of a boulder result (same `setDoc` call as `updatedAt`), so it never actually meant "when was this first validated." That was harmless while nothing read it, but the season reconciliation above needs exactly that meaning to decide which season an old validation belongs to. `ClientDaily.tsx`'s `resolvePreviousResultState` (renamed from the classement-only `resolvePreviousClassementState`) now reads the existing document's `createdAt` before every write and carries it forward unchanged; `updatedAt` still updates every time. This read now runs unconditionally (previously gated on the boulder having an active color), since `createdAt` must be preserved even for boulders without a resolvable color. Verified (ClaudeNav review, 2026-08-17) to be the *only* two write sites for this collection project-wide — no admin screen, migration script, or other path writes it.
- **Roster generation is a manual admin action, not automatic**: a "Générer le roster" button (`AdminCompetitionManagement.tsx`, shown only for `scoring_mode === 'officiel'` competitions, next to "Saisie juge") reads every `classement_saisons` doc and picks the most recent by sorting IDs client-side (`"YYYY-YYYY"` compares lexicographically) — **not** `orderBy('__name__', 'desc')`, which Firestore rejects ("does not support descending key scans"), found by the e2e pass — and bulk-creates `competition_participants` for each qualified uid not already registered, with `?? null` on every optional field copied from `users` (a `setDoc` throws on `undefined`, not `null`; also fixed in the manual-add path, `AdminCompetitionRegistration.tsx`, same bug). It only seeds the roster; nothing prevents the admin from adding/removing individual participants afterward through the existing screen.

#### Season restart during rollout (V2.56) — `season.*` becomes a monotone accumulator, and the season calc drops the `is_active` filter

The season launched with the public rollout and is unfair while most climbers aren't on the app yet. `AdminSeasonConfig.tsx` gained a **"Redémarrer la saison maintenant"** button (Modèle A, `docs/handoffs/HANDOFF-redemarrage-saison-2026-09-06.md` + `docs/handoffs/RETOUR-redemarrage-saison-modele-a.md`). It is admin-operable, browser-side (admin can already write any `classement_profiles` — `isUserRole("admin")` in rules, no rules change), idempotent (dotted-path writes replace, a second click recomputes the same thing).

- **What it does, in order (profiles first, config last — deliberate):** ① load *all* daily boulders (`type=='daily'`, **no `is_active` filter**); ② for every `classement_profiles` doc, recompute `season.baseScore`/`season.baseColorCounts` **and** `season.score`/`season.colorCounts` from that climber's own `client_boulder_results` (`success:true`, original `attempts`, each boulder at its current/last-known colour) via the pure `recomputeSeasonBaseline` in `classementScore.ts`; ③ **only then** write `app_config/classement_saison` with the new `[debut, fin]` + `cloturee:false`. The reverse order would let a reconciliation pass on a not-yet-recomputed profile compute an amputated expected and write it.
- **`season.baseScore`/`season.baseColorCounts` are the frozen starting credit.** `season.score` = `base` + Σ(validations since `debut`); the incremental delta path (`ClientDaily.tsx`) adds to `season.score`/`colorCounts` as before, `base*` is never touched again until the next restart or the end-of-season close.
- **The season is now a monotone accumulator — points never go down when a wall rotates.** This is the whole point of the restart ("c'est l'idée même du changement"). Consequences:
  - **`ClientDaily.tsx`'s season gate now tests the validation's own `createdAt`, not "today"** (`applyClassementDelta(... resultCreatedAt ...)`). After a restart the entire pre-`debut` history is out-of-window, so editing/un-validating such a boulder must NOT move `season.*` (its credit lives frozen in `base*`) — the old "today ∈ window" gate would have pushed the score below the base credit. **But the `createdAt` test alone is not enough** (ClaudeNav review 2026-09-06): during the close→reconfigure gap (`cloturee: true`, up to 7 days), the window `[debut, fin]` is still the finished season's, so a validation whose `createdAt` falls in it would re-populate the just-zeroed counters. Fixed by having `fetchSeasonWindow` leave `seasonWindowRef` at `null` when `cloturee === true` (mirrors `loadSeasonWindow()`'s `if (data.cloturee) return null` server-side) — no season delta is applied at all while a season is closed. e2e-season-restart-flow.mjs step 10 exercises exactly this (validate a boulder after close → `season.*` stays 0, before and after `reconcile --fix`).
  - **`reconcile-classement-profiles.js` no longer filters on `is_active`** — one `loadDailyColorById` (`type=='daily'` only) serves *both* the all-time and the season pass. Expected season value = `storedBase + Σ(in-window validations, each boulder active-or-not, at its last-known colour)`. This keeps the V2.42 two-way reconciliation (no "floor", no "log-only") — the trick was making the expected calculable by storing `base*` and dropping the activity filter. **If a profile has no `season.baseScore` yet (no restart since V2.56 deployed), the reconciliation skips `season.*` for it entirely** (`computeExpectedProfile` returns `seasonScore: undefined`).
  - The revert vs V2.42: reconciliation used to strip removed-boulder contributions from *both* counters; now it keeps them for both (see the incremental-counter section above). The season pass additionally uses `base*` + the window filter. **First `--fix` after deploying V2.56 will *raise* the all-time score of any climber who validated a since-retired boulder** (restoring points wrongly stripped by earlier runs) — real drift for the guard, one `--force` confirms it, same as any mass realignment.
- **`compute-classement-saison.js` (end-of-season close) now zeros `season.baseScore`/`baseColorCounts` too**, else next season starts with the old credit. It also switched its reset from `batch.set(..., {merge:true})` (which does *not* empty a nested `colorCounts` map — the V2.53 orphan-key bug, latent here, never triggered since no season had closed) to `batch.update` with dotted paths (`'season.colorCounts': {}` genuinely clears it; safe because `fetchAllProfiles` gives a query snapshot so every doc exists).
- **Accounts with validations but no `classement_profiles` doc are not seeded by the button** (it iterates `classement_profiles`, not `users`) — they get their baseline on their next validation, or from the next `reconcile --fix`. In prod this is a non-case (profiles were backfilled in V2.35). Same "a climber who joined after `debut` has no starting credit" property, which is correct.
